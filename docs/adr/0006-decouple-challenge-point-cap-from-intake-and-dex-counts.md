# Decouple Daily Challenge Point Cap From Intake Counts and Flavor Dex Unlocks

Volume-based Challenges enforce a configurable `Daily Point Cap` defaulting to 1 counted can per calendar day so participants are never incentivized to over-consume caffeine to win a leaderboard. However, this cap applies strictly to volume-based Challenge point calculation: a second or third can logged on the same day still posts to the Club Timeline, increments Personal, Club, and Global Flavor Counts truthfully, and unlocks new slots in the Personal, Club, and Challenge Flavor Dex.

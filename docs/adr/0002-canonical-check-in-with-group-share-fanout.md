# Canonical Check-In With Group-Share Fan-Out

Each physical Monster can logged by a Member is stored as a single canonical `CheckIn` record owned by that Member, and is linked to Clubs and Challenges via separate `GroupShare` records. A Member may belong to several Clubs and participate in simultaneous Club and Community Challenges; separating the canonical intake event from its group projections ensures a single photo check-in fans out to every relevant feed and leaderboard while guaranteeing personal and Global flavor counts are never double-counted.
